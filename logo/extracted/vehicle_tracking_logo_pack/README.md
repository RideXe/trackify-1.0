# Vehicle Tracking App — Logo Pack

This pack is based on the supplied “03 · Bold” vehicle-tracking icon reference.

## Contents

- `master/vehicle-tracking-icon-1024.png` — 1024×1024 master PNG
- `app-icons/` — common square PNG icon sizes, plus iOS App Store and Android Play Store-sized copies
- `web/` — PWA icons and PNG favicons
- `reference/original-reference.png` — original uploaded reference screenshot

## Notes

- The icon artwork was cropped from the supplied screenshot and upscaled with high-quality resampling.
- These are raster PNG assets. Upscaling cannot recover detail that was not present in the screenshot.
- The artwork includes the rounded-square background from the reference; it is not a transparent-background logo.
- Platform submission requirements can change. Confirm current requirements before publishing.
